Some Questions & Answers

01

What services do you offer for book publishing?

We provide a range of publishing services, including manuscript editing, book formatting, ISBN registration, and global distribution. Our team ensures that your book meets industry standards for print and digital formats.

02

How do you help with manuscript editing?

Our professional editing team works closely with you to enhance the clarity, structure, and flow of your manuscript. We offer comprehensive editing and proofreading services to ensure your book is polished and ready for publication.

03

What is the process for getting an ISBN?

We assist with ISBN registration to ensure that your book is properly identified in the global market. Our team will guide you through the process and help secure your ISBN for both print and digital formats.

04

Do you assist with copyright protection?

Yes, we help authors understand and navigate copyright protection for their work. We offer guidance on how to legally protect your book from unauthorized use.

05

Which publishing platforms do you use?

We support a variety of publishing platforms, including a img, IngramSpark, and other industry-standard digital and print services. We work with you to choose the best platform for your book’s needs.

06

Is online book marketing for me?

Online book marketing can be beneficial for any author looking to increase visibility. We tailor our marketing strategies to suit your book’s genre and target audience, whether you're just starting out or looking to expand your reach.

07

What competitive advantages will I get from book marketing?

Our book marketing services are designed to help you reach the right audience, increase your book's visibility, and ultimately drive sales. We use data-driven strategies, social media outreach, and email campaigns to ensure your book stands out.

08

Can you design my book cover?

Yes, our design team creates custom book covers that are professional, eye-catching, and aligned with your book's genre and theme.

09

What kind of marketing support do you provide?

We offer a comprehensive marketing package, including social media promotion, email campaigns, book reviews, and other strategies to increase your book’s reach and build a loyal reader base.

10

Do you offer print-on-demand services?

Yes, we offer print-on-demand services, allowing you to print copies of your book as needed. This is an efficient and cost-effective solution for authors looking to minimize upfront printing costs.

11

How long does it take to publish a book?

The timeline varies depending on the complexity of your project. Typically, the process can take anywhere from a few weeks to a few months, depending on the services you require.

12

What is your pricing structure?

Our pricing varies depending on the services you choose. We offer packages that are designed to meet different needs and budgets. Contact us for a customized quote based on your specific requirements.

13

What if I don’t need all your services? Can I only pay for specific services?

Yes, we offer flexibility in our services. You can select only the services you need, and we will tailor a package to fit your exact requirements.

14

I do not have any social media accounts. How can you help?

No worries! We can assist in creating and managing social media accounts for your book, as well as help with promotional strategies to build your online presence.
